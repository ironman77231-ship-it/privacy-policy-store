Privacy Policy for Taobao Shopping Cart Assistant - One-click Export Product List

*Last updated November 5, 2026*

This policy explains how the Taobao Shopping Cart Assistant Chrome extension handles information. It applies to version 5.2.2. The extension is an independent shopping tool and has no affiliation with or endorsement from Taobao or Alibaba Group.

## 1. Shopping cart data and exports

The extension reads product information from the shopping cart page to display the items and fields you can export. This can include product names, prices, specifications, quantities, and product or store links. When you export a selection, the extension generates the Excel or CSV file in your browser and saves it to your device. Export activity may generate a usage event with counts of selected items or fields.

## 2. Image search and screenshot translation

When you request an image search or screenshot translation, the extension may send the selected image, its URL, or the captured part of the screen to AiPrice services or the search provider used for the request. The request may include the product or page context needed to return results or the language needed to translate the screenshot. The recipient uses this information to process the requested feature.

## 3. Cookies, settings, and analytics

- **Cookies and site settings.** The extension can read or update site cookies for features that depend on an active session or site settings such as currency. It uses browser storage for extension preferences, including language and feature settings.
- **Usage and diagnostics.** The extension may send feature events and technical details to its Matomo analytics endpoint and to Google Analytics to measure usage and diagnose problems. Depending on the event, these details may include an extension or browser identifier, extension version, browser language, page URL or title, and the number of items or fields involved in an action.

## 4. Browser permissions

The extension requests access to websites so its content scripts can recognize supported shopping pages and so tools such as screenshot search and translation can work on the page you choose. It uses `activeTab` and `scripting` to run page features, `contextMenus` to provide shortcuts, and `storage` to save settings. The `cookies` permission supports the session and settings functions described above. The extension also requests permissions for scheduled tasks, notifications, and browser rules that manage feature requests. The optional `downloads` permission supports features that save files to your device.

## 5. Data sharing and retention

AiPrice and the search or analytics services described above receive the information needed to provide those features or measure how they work. We do not sell personal data or use shopping cart contents or screenshots for personalized advertising.

Exported files remain on your device until you delete them. You can clear locally stored extension settings by removing the extension. For questions about information sent to a service, including retention or requests to access or delete personal data, contact the publisher using the address below.
