# Privacy Policy for Shopping Cart Export Assistant for 1688 (Independent Third-Party Version)

*Last updated November 10, 2026*

This policy describes how the Chrome extension “购物车一键导出助手 for 1688 (独立第三方版)” handles information.

## 1. Scope of Data Access

The cart export feature reads product information on the 1688 shopping cart page. The extension has broader website access for supported page tools, including screenshot search and translation.

## 2. Information Collection and Use

- **Shopping cart data and CSV export.** When you use the cart export feature, the extension reads product details such as names, prices, variants, quantities, and product or store links. It generates a CSV file in the browser and saves it to your device.
- **Cookies and settings.** The extension can access site cookies for supported features that depend on sign-in status or site settings, such as currency. It uses browser storage to save extension preferences.
- **Image search and translation.** When you use these tools, the extension may send a selected image, image URL, or screenshot and related page context to AiPrice or the service provider that processes the request.
- **Usage analytics.** The extension may send usage events and technical information to Matomo and Google Analytics. Depending on the event, this may include a browser or extension identifier, extension version, browser language, page URL or title, and counts of selected items or export fields.

## 3. Browser Permissions

The extension uses tab and page access to recognize supported shopping pages and run page tools, the browser context menu to offer shortcuts, cookie access for supported site functions, and local storage for extension settings. It also requests permissions for scheduled tasks, notifications, and browser network rules. Optional download access supports tools that save files to your device.

## 4. Independent Third-Party Disclaimer

The extension is independently developed. 1688 and Alibaba Group do not operate, sponsor, or endorse it.

## 5. Data Sharing and Retention

AiPrice, search and translation providers, and analytics services may receive the information described above to process feature requests or measure usage. Exported CSV files remain on your device until you delete them. You can clear locally stored extension settings by removing the extension. For questions about retention of information sent to a service, contact the publisher.
